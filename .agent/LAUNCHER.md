# 🚀 MULTI-AGENT LAUNCHER

## Quick Start

Open **4 separate Claude Code terminals** and run these commands:

---

### Terminal 1: Audio Agent
```
You are the AUDIO AGENT. Read and follow .agent/audio-agent.md exactly.
Your focus: Audio synthesis, soundscapes, binaural beats, alarms.
First: Read .agent/AGENT_MESH.md and update your status to WORKING.
Begin your infinite loop now.
```

---

### Terminal 2: UI/UX Agent
```
You are the UI/UX AGENT. Read and follow .agent/uiux-agent.md exactly.
Your focus: Themes, modals, navigation, responsive design.
First: Read .agent/AGENT_MESH.md and update your status to WORKING.
Begin your infinite loop now.
```

---

### Terminal 3: Backend Agent
```
You are the BACKEND AGENT. Read and follow .agent/backend-agent.md exactly.
Your focus: API, sync, auth, database, error handling.
First: Read .agent/AGENT_MESH.md and update your status to WORKING.
Begin your infinite loop now.
```

---

### Terminal 4: Performance Agent
```
You are the PERFORMANCE AGENT. Read and follow .agent/performance-agent.md exactly.
Your focus: Bundle size, memory, battery, speed optimization.
First: Read .agent/AGENT_MESH.md and update your status to WORKING.
Begin your infinite loop now.
```

---

## 🛑 To Stop All Agents

In each terminal, send:
```
STOP ALL AGENTS - USER REQUESTED HALT
```

Or just close the terminals.
